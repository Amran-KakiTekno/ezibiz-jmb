# 🏛️ EziBiz JMB (Komuniti)
> **Modern Residential Strata Management, Financial Transparency & Neighborhood Social Platform**  
> Tailored for Malaysian Joint Management Bodies (JMB), Management Corporations (MC), and Strata Residents under the **Strata Management Act 2013 (Act 757)**.

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Anti-Songlap Glassbox Transparency Ledger**: Publicly visible sinking fund reserves runway and itemized, contractor-verified disbursements to build community trust.
2. **Statutory Act 757 Separation & Calculation**:
   - Automated separation of Maintenance Account vs Sinking Fund (10% statutory minimum).
   - 10% per annum simple late interest calculation.
   - Statutory **Borang 28 / Form 28** Notice generator for inter-floor water leakage disputes under Regulation 56 of the Strata Management Regulations 2015.
3. **Zero-Install Mobile PWA + WhatsApp Nudge**:
   - 1-Click WhatsApp Dunning Link generator for the management office.
   - Instant DuitNow QR & FPX simulated payment with official printable A4 tax e-Receipts.
4. **Pasar Komuniti & Social Harmony Hub**:
   - Resident marketplace (preloved goods, home bakes, services).
   - Carpool & parking bay rental exchange.
   - Lost & found registry.
   - Official pinned notices with read receipts.
5. **Guardhouse Security Terminal**:
   - 1-Click shareable Visitor QR Passes.
   - Guardhouse barrier boom gate verification scanner.
   - Parcel intake & arrival logger with auto-notifications.
